App Support

Welcome to the support page for Car Finder (Poland) App.

About the App

Car Finder (Poland) is an app that simplifies car searching on Polish online services.
Thanks to the app, you select the criteria for a search once, and the app does the searching and parsing for you everytime you start the app, refresh it or even in the background, if the iOS executes the scheduled task once an hour.

Frequently Asked Questions

The app is not displaying any offers. What should I do? Make sure you have selected proper filters for offers to show.
You can chose to display offers that you have marked as Accepted ✅, Denied ❌ (by default hiden) or Not sure ?. How can I restore my purchases? If the app supports in-app purchases, use the Restore Purchases option in the app, if available.
