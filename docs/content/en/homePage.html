Welcome to my home.

I wanted to create a portfolio page where I could present what I do at work, but as always I had no time to do it. Working full time while having three small kids is challenging task. Especially if you live in a small village, and you have to do everything around your house yourself :).

Moreover, recently I decided, that I need a hobby and bought a dream for myself.
And Boy! It is a truly expensive hobby :)...
You can see it in a logo and launch screen for my Car Finder App. It is 1985.5 Porsche 944 N/A, and it is a real dream to drive, but just like all the other years old cars, it requires a lot of attention... and love.

I know how hard is to find a car that will suit your all exaggerated expectations, and how much time it may take to go through all those offer boards on the Internet.
What is more frustrating, all that searching takes lots of time, especially if the order of offers changes, the promoted offers occupy the top of the page, and you end up going multiple times to the same car, you don't want to see again. Or you can't find that one you were really interested in, but it went somewhere deep in the dooms of Internet.
That is why I started this project. I started with a Chrome extension, that I perfected for quite a long time, while looking for a car for my wife.
Then I thought: "Most people do use their phones way more often than computers, and they have the phone with them all the time."
And that was when I typed first commands to my app. During development, a lot of ideas morphed and I finally ended changing things after things. I hope you like what I have created, and that you could use it for your own benefit.
