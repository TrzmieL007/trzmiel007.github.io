This Privacy Policy describes how CarFinder ("the App") handles information when you use the App.

The App uses Google AdMob, a mobile advertising service provided by Google LLC, to display advertisements.

AdMob may collect and process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, approximate location, and information about interactions with advertisements and the App. The information processed may depend on your consent and the applicable advertising settings.

The App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain and manage consent where required by applicable privacy laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Depending on your choices and applicable requirements, Google may display personalized, non-personalized, or limited advertisements.
